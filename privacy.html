Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Super App. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect your username, email, phone number, and profile picture when you create an account. We also collect media (videos, photos, voice notes) that you upload.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide app features (like messaging, live streaming, and posting), to process payments, and to show you relevant ads (via Google AdMob).

3. Third-Party Services

We use Firebase for database and authentication, Catbox.moe for media hosting, Agora for live streaming, and Google AdMob for advertisements. These services have their own privacy policies.

4. Data Deletion

You can delete your posts or account at any time. If you need help, contact us at umarsuperapp@gmail.com.
